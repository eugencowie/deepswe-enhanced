# OpenAI doubles ChatGPT Pro limits, 2026-10-29

Transcribed on 2026-10-31 from [Thibault Sottiaux's post on X](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115850175868727621), posted 2026-10-29 at 4:56 pm. Sottiaux leads Codex and ChatGPT Work at OpenAI. [Ticket 10](../tickets/10-chatgpt-pro-limits-doubled.md) uses it.

## The post

> Day 25/
>
> We are **doubling all usage limits for all Pro 100/200/500 tiers and Business Premium, effective today**.
>
> We heard your kind feedback and won't be reducing usage limits for Pro 200, so your Work and Codex usage will stay the same. We're also going to continue offering extended limits for dots for now, and usage of your dot will still continue to be separate from your default usage.
>
> And we'll of course also keep shipping better models and other efficiency improvements that increase your effective usage over time.

## What it changes

- **Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500** carry a limit adjustment of 2 on every model's measured value. A month is capped by the weekly meters ([SemiAnalysis method](semianalysis-2026-10-05.md#method)), and the post doubles "all usage limits", so the monthly value doubles too.
- **Plus** isn't named and is unchanged.
- **Business Premium** and **dots** aren't tiers we model. Dots usage is metered apart from default usage anyway.
- **Permanent.** The post gives no end date, unlike Z.ai's dated promos. If OpenAI later dates it, the adjustment goes.

## Pro 200

On 2026-09-29 OpenAI halved Pro 200's limits, keeping the old limits for existing subscribers until 2026-10-29 ([SemiAnalysis, pre-cut Pro 200](semianalysis-2026-10-05.md#pre-cut-pro-200-not-used)). The post can be read three ways:

1. The post-cut limits are doubled, which is about the pre-cut level (GPT-6 Astra: 2 × $2,897 = $5,794 against $5,734 measured pre-cut).
2. The cut is cancelled, which is the pre-cut level again.
3. The cut is cancelled and the pre-cut limits are doubled, about 4× post-cut.

Readings 1 and 2 agree, and "your Work and Codex usage will stay the same" rules out 3. Pro 200 takes 2× its post-cut values. Its measured pre-cut figures aren't used: the Sol figure ($5,904) is GPT-6 Sol's, before GPT-6.1 Sol replaced it.

Coverage disagrees on whether Pro 200 was doubled or only spared the cut: [heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/OpenAI-doubles-select-usage-limits-and-updates-AI-goals-11506557.html) says only Pro 100, Pro 500 and Business Premium are doubled, [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/30/openai-doubles-chatgpt-pro-and-business-premium-usage-limits-after-dropping-a-planned-pro-200-cut/) that all three Pro tiers are. Either way Pro 200 lands at about its pre-cut level. OpenAI publishes no numeric limits, so nobody can check the doubling against a figure.

## Why a limit adjustment

The values in `tiers.json` stay SemiAnalysis's, and the ×2 sits beside them with its own citation:

- **Baking the doubled values into `apiEquivalentValuesUsdPerMonth`** is simpler, but those numbers would match no SemiAnalysis chart, so a reviewer couldn't check them against [the transcription](semianalysis-2026-10-05.md).
- **The usage multiplier ADR 0010 deleted** was a per-model factor we fitted ourselves. A limit adjustment is per tier and stated by the vendor, so it scales every model on the tier alike.

When SemiAnalysis measures the Pro tiers after 2026-10-29, the new values replace the old ones and the adjustment is deleted.
